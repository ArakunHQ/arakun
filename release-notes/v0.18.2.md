## Faster macOS launch, lower idle power and smoother sessions

- **macOS:** Opening Arakun from Finder or the Dock is noticeably faster, without waiting on your login shell before the window appears.
- **Agents:** Typing stays responsive while several sessions stream in parallel, long agent runs open and scroll smoothly, and new sessions start with fewer prompt tokens.
- **App:** Idle background wakeups across the window and Dynamic Island are reduced so Arakun uses less battery at rest.
- **Fixes:** Feedback sent from the app arrives as clean plain text.
