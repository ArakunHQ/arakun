## Sign in to reach your computers, and side-by-side tabs

- **Phone:** Your phone reaches your computers through the relay by signing in with GitHub, not with a pairing link. Sign in once in the Phone tab and on your phone with the same account, then pick a computer. Phones paired by link must sign in again. On a server, run `arakun-server login`.
- **Marketplace:** A skill's details list the other skills in its repository, with Install all. You can also search for a repository by `owner/repo` or a GitHub link. Marketplace and Cloudflare show their pages as tabs across the top of the view.
- **Tabs:** You can split tabs from two environments side by side, and clicking a pane switches to its environment.
- **Speed:** Arakun opens faster from Finder or the Dock on macOS. Typing stays quick while several sessions stream, long agent runs scroll smoothly, and Arakun uses less battery while idle.
- **Models:** The model menu shows new models after Antigravity or Cursor updates. After a subagent runs, a new Claude session still offers every model and reasoning level.
- **Fixes:** Browser tabs show the site's icon more often, also for tabs not opened since a restart. Small images you send show at their real size. Typing / in a server's environment lists your mirrored skills right away. On macOS, saved keys go to the Keychain.
