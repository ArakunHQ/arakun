## Computers tab and a session tree you order by hand

- **Computers:** The Servers tab is now Computers: this computer and your servers in one list, each with its status, environments and latest sessions. Remove takes a server off in one step, and a server signed in to your account shows up by itself.
- **Sidebar:** Pick the session order from a menu, or drag sessions into your own order, for every project or only some. Pinned sessions and sessions with an open tab come first, and the rest are dimmed.
- **Fixes:** Closing the window from the system, such as with Alt+F4 or on Windows, now saves your sessions and installs a downloaded update. On Linux, Arakun no longer closes by itself after hours of use with the built-in browser.
