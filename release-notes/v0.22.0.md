## Running servers, phone app betas and notifications

- **Running:** The status bar shows how many local dev servers run and how much memory they use. Click it to see each one by project, open its port, jump to its terminal or stop it.
- **Phone apps:** You can ask to join the iOS or Android beta from the new Phone app beta section in the Phone tab. The iOS and Android apps tell you when a session finishes, needs your answer or fails, and you pick which of these you get.
- **Phone page:** On a tablet or a desktop browser the phone page shows your sessions in a sidebar beside the open one. It follows the brand look, and its light theme now follows your system setting.
- **Relay:** The free plan allows 10 computers, 5 phones and 100,000 frames a day.
- **macOS:** Opened from Finder or the Dock, agents get your shell's PATH even when your shell setup prints a lot.
