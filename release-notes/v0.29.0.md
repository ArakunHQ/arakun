## Three panes, PDFs for Claude and subagents on your phone

- **Sessions:** A tab can show up to three panes side by side, and Claude reads attached PDFs directly. The **+** button opens your system's file picker, and Settings › Branches › Remove archived sessions after deletes old archived sessions for you.
- **Phone:** The phone page and the iPhone and Android apps show a session's subagents and their transcripts, pictures and formatted replies, and the iPhone app attaches photos to a prompt. You can take back a queued prompt, resume a paused queue, and stop and send from every phone app.
- **Linear:** A delegated issue with an open pull request continues on it, and the session tells you when its checks fail or a reviewer asks for changes. When no computer takes an issue, Linear asks which project to work in. Cloud access and Linear need a linked GitHub account.
- **Sidebar:** The new View strip preset puts views in a thin strip beside your sessions, and Needs you first is the default layout. Finished sessions fold into one line per project, and Alt+1 to Alt+9 open a project's sessions.
- **Look:** Tabs, the sidebar, the Workspace panel, dialogs and new blocks in a session ease in and out, and the dot of a session that needs you blinks. The window draws faster.
- **Fixes:** Settings › Account lets you add and remove sign-in methods. The Marketplace shows each MCP server once and names where an item installs. Private files stay private on Windows.
